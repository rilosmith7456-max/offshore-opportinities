Official Coast Guard Process

The current NMC guidance identifies application types including Original, Renewal, Raise of Grade, Modification/Increase of Scope, STCW, Duplicate, Replacement and Document of Continuity. NMC guidance also directs applicants to use current forms and its current submission system. Your website should link clients to the official NMC information rather than presenting your business as the credentialing authority.
